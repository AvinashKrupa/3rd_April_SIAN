How It Works

A comprehensive framework to discover, nurture, and scale startups across South India through six interconnected pillars.

Unified Digital Platform

One platform connecting all aspects of the startup journey - from discovery to scale.

Discovery

Identify and showcase promising startups from across South India through our comprehensive discovery engine.

Networking

Connect founders with investors, mentors, and fellow entrepreneurs through our integrated networking features.

Mentorship

Access experienced mentors who provide guidance on business strategy, technology, and growth.

Funding

Connect with angel investors, VCs, and funding programs suited to your startup's stage and needs.

Building

Access resources, tools, and support to build and iterate on your product and business model.

Learning

Continuous learning through workshops, courses, and knowledge resources tailored for founders.

Startup Summits & Discovery Drives

Regional summits and grassroots discovery missions that bring the ecosystem together and identify promising startups from every corner of South India.

  • State-level flagship summits connecting founders, investors, and ecosystem players
  • District-level discovery drives reaching Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities
  • University roadshows engaging student entrepreneurs
  • Sector-specific meetups and demo days

Incubation & Acceleration

Tailored support programs for startups at every stage of their journey.

1

Idea Stage

Validate your concept, build initial prototypes, and refine your value proposition with expert guidance.

2

Early Stage

Develop your MVP, find product-market fit, and build your initial customer base with structured support.

3

Growth Stage

Scale operations, expand markets, and prepare for funding rounds with acceleration programs.

Capital & Advisory

Connecting startups with diverse funding sources and strategic advisors.

Angel Investors

Access to SIAN's network of 500+ angel investors who provide early-stage capital and mentorship.

Venture Capital

Connections with leading VC firms focused on Series A and beyond investments in promising startups.

Family Offices

Engage with family offices interested in strategic investments and long-term partnerships.

Government Funds

Navigate state and central government funding schemes, grants, and startup incentive programs.

Policy Advocacy

Working with governments across South India to create startup-friendly policies that enable innovation and entrepreneurship to thrive.

  • Startup policy recommendations and advocacy
  • Regulatory simplification initiatives
  • Public-private partnership facilitation
  • Ease-of-doing-business improvements

Learning & Skill Hubs

Continuous education and skill development for founders and their teams.

Bootcamps

Intensive programs on key topics like fundraising, product development, and go-to-market strategies.

Workshops

Hands-on sessions covering practical skills from financial modeling to customer acquisition.

Partner Institutions

Collaborations with leading business schools and universities for structured learning programs.
