Startup Discovery Drives

Grassroots missions to identify and nurture promising startups from every district, town, and village across South India - ensuring no innovative idea goes unnoticed.

What We Offer:

  • On-ground discovery missions in Tier 2 & Tier 3 cities
  • University and college startup identification programs
  • Rural innovation scouting initiatives
  • Direct pathway to incubation and funding
