Our Regional Footprint

Five states and union territories with unique strengths, united in the mission to ignite a million startups.

Karnataka

India's Deeptech & Startup Capital

Home to India's largest startup ecosystem, Karnataka leads in SaaS, deeptech, and enterprise innovation with Bengaluru as its crown jewel.

Focus Sectors:
SaaS & Enterprise Tech Deeptech & AI Biotech & Healthtech Climate & Sustainability
Key Cities: Bengaluru, Mysuru, Hubballi, Mangaluru
Tamil Nadu

Manufacturing, Innovation & Scale

A manufacturing powerhouse transitioning into Industry 4.0, with strong EV, hardware, and IoT innovation clusters.

Focus Sectors:
Manufacturing & Industry 4.0 EV & Mobility Hardware & IoT EdTech & HealthTech
Key Cities: Chennai, Coimbatore, Madurai, Trichy
Telangana

Policy-led Innovation & Global Outlook

Leading with progressive startup policies, T-Hub, and a thriving pharma & life sciences ecosystem centered in Hyderabad.

Focus Sectors:
AI & Data Platforms Agritech MedTech & Life Sciences GovTech
Key Cities: Hyderabad, Warangal, Karimnagar
Andhra Pradesh

Emerging Founders & Coastal Innovation

A rapidly emerging ecosystem with strengths in agritech, logistics, and fintech, leveraging its strategic coastal location.

Focus Sectors:
Agritech Logistics & Ports FinTech Renewable Energy
Key Cities: Visakhapatnam, Vijayawada, Tirupati
Kerala

Knowledge Economy & Sustainable Innovation

A highly educated talent pool driving innovation in deeptech, healthcare, and sustainable tourism technology.

Focus Sectors:
Deeptech & R&D HealthTech EdTech Tourism & Sustainability
Key Cities: Kochi, Trivandrum, Kozhikode
