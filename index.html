How We Catalyse Innovation

A comprehensive framework to discover, nurture, and scale startups across South India through six interconnected pillars.

1

Unified Digital Platform

Discovery, networking, mentorship, funding, building, and learning - all in one place.

2

Startup Summits & Discovery Drives

Regional summits and grassroots discovery missions to identify promising ventures.

3

Incubation & Acceleration

Support for idea stage, early stage, and growth stage startups with tailored programs.

4

Capital & Advisory

Access to Angels, VCs, Family Offices, and Government Funds for startup growth.

5

Policy Advocacy

Working towards startup-friendly policies across all South Indian states.

6

Learning & Skill Hubs

About The Initiative

An Initiative of South India Angel Network (SIAN)

Startup Dakshina Bharata is powered by SIAN's extensive network of investors, mentors, and industry leaders committed to fostering entrepreneurship across South India.

Together, we are building a vibrant, interconnected startup ecosystem rooted in regional strengths and global aspirations.
